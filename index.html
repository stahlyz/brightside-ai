About

Brightside AI helps small businesses, schools, universities, and nonprofits put AI to work in ways that are practical, safe, and affordable.

Hi, I'm Josh Whitaker, an independent AI consultant and the person behind Brightside AI. I'll help you figure out where AI can save your team time, set it up safely, and train your people to use it with confidence.

I've spent 20 years in IT, including 11+ in cybersecurity, so I know how to keep technology running and keep it safe. I've administered systems and networks for a school district on a tight budget, which taught me to get real results from practical, often open-source tools. These days I build with AI tools like Claude and experiment with open-source AI models in my own home lab.

I like clear answers over buzzwords, and I'll always explain what I'm building and why. A person stays in charge of the decisions that matter. If you're curious about AI but not sure where to start, let's talk.
